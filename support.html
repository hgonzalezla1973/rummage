In the app: Account → Send feedback

Reach out for bug reports, account issues, listing problems, or anything else — screenshots help a lot if something looks wrong. For general questions about how Rummage works, check the Help & FAQ page first, since it covers the most common ones instantly.

The app can't find my location

Make sure Location Services are turned on for Rummage: on your device, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Rummage, and set it to "While Using the App." You can also skip this and enter your ZIP code manually from the Search tab.

I'm not getting the daily digest or notifications

Confirm Daily digest email and/or Push notifications are turned on in Account → Notifications, and that you're signed in with an email address (guest accounts can't receive email or push). Also check your spam folder for the digest email.

My listing isn't showing up

New listings appear on the map immediately, but double-check your sale's dates haven't already passed and that your address geocoded correctly — try re-entering it if the map pin looks off. If it still isn't showing after a few minutes, email us with your listing's title and we'll take a look.

I want my data deleted

Email us at the address above with the account email address you'd like removed, and we'll take care of it. See our Privacy Policy for more on what we collect and how it's used.
