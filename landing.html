Why Rummage

Stop checking five sites before breakfast.

Yard sale listings are scattered across Facebook, Nextdoor, and a dozen estate-sale directories — each with its own format, and none of them talk to each other. Rummage does the aggregating for you.

One map, every source

EstateSales.org, EstateSales.net, GSALR, Nextdoor, and more — deduplicated and dropped onto a single live map, sorted by distance from wherever you are.

Alerts for what you're after

Pick your interests — furniture, tools, vinyl records, and more — and get a push notification the moment a nearby sale lists something that matches.

Plan your route

Add sales to your trip and get turn-by-turn directions between every stop — built for a Saturday morning circuit, not a single address.

Search anywhere

Traveling? Rummage detects when you're away from your home ZIP and automatically searches near where you actually are.

List your own sale

Running a sale this weekend? Post it in under a minute — free — and it shows up on the map alongside everything else immediately.

Daily digest, if you want it

Opt in to a morning email with that day's sales near you — or skip it entirely and just browse. Your call.
