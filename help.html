Rummage — Help & FAQ

Answers to the most common questions. Still stuck? See our Support page.

How do I find sales near me?

Open the Explore tab to see a map of all nearby sales. Use the Search tab to set your ZIP code and radius. The map updates automatically as you adjust your area.

How do I save a sale?

Tap the heart icon on any sale card or detail page. Saved sales appear in your Saved tab so you can find them quickly later.

How do I list my own sale?

Tap the List tab at the bottom of the screen. Fill in your sale type, address, dates, hours, and a brief description. Your listing goes live on the map immediately.

Can I get sale listings sent to me?

Yes. Turn on Daily digest email in Account → Notifications and we'll email you that day's sales near your ZIP code each morning. You can also set up Interests in Account to get a push notification when a nearby sale lists something you're hunting for.

How do I change my search radius?

Go to the Search tab and drag the radius slider, or tap the preset buttons (5, 10, 25, 50 mi). You can also update your default radius in Account → Search defaults.

How do I contact a seller?

Open a sale listing and scroll to the contact section. Tap "Contact Seller" to send the seller a message using the contact method they provided.

Is Rummage free?

Yes — Rummage is completely free for shoppers. Listing your own sale is also free.

Why don't I see sales in my area?

Try widening your search radius or removing active type filters. Sales are also filtered to show only upcoming and current listings, so past sales won't appear.
